# World model credits

- **Hand-made CC0 models by Quaternius** (quaternius.com, CC0 1.0 public domain): Stylized Nature MegaKit (trees,
  bushes, grass, flowers, rocks, mushrooms), Fantasy Props MegaKit (barrels, crates, stalls, anvil, cauldron "kettle",
  weapon rack, training dummy, bench, workbench, bucket, farm crate, cage, torch, wall lantern, and the coin pile and
  loot pouch that pop out of monsters as loot_coins / loot_pouch) and Medieval Village
  MegaKit (wooden fence, wagon, the lantern post's beam and stones). Converted with tools/v11_kit.cjs,
