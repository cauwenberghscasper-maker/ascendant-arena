# Research: a more rewarding game, a home farm, a non-linear world (24 Oct 2026)

Asked by Casper: the game needs a more rewarding feeling; look at popular games (Archero / arrow.io and other
fantasy games) for what keeps players coming back; consider an extra place outside the world (a farm); make the world
non-linear, because walking north the whole time feels boring. **This is research only: nothing in the game changed.**
Every idea below keeps the README rules (no pay-to-win, no paid chests, odds shown, real models only, saves never
break, gentle unlocks, no walls of text).

## 1. What we have today (and why it feels flat)

- **Lots of systems, one direction.** 29 areas sit in one 3.5 km line north. Level N means "the next band". The
  only choice the world asks is "keep going?". Once an area is out-levelled there is no reason to return.
- **Rewards are mostly numbers in menus.** Kills give XP and drops; the exciting moments (chest reels, Level Road
  milestones) happen in panels, not in the world while you fight.
- **Few short goals on screen.** Every session moves a long bar (level, mastery), but there is rarely a "3 more
  kills and you get X" goal you can finish in two minutes.
- **No place that is yours and grows** besides the Bastion fort (level 20, a tower defense, not a calm home).

## 2. What competitors do

| Game | The hook we can learn from | Fit for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Archero / Archero 2** (Habby) | Short runs; a choice of 1 of 3 skills on every level-up inside a run; a simple meta layer on top; systems revealed only when the player needs them (Habby reported +33% retention from paced unlocks). | High: run-based skill picks inside dungeons and the Rift. We already pace unlocks. |
| **arrow.io / .io brawlers** | Instant power growth in the match (eat, grow, upgrade on the spot), a live leaderboard, 1-minute rounds. | Medium: Showdown could get in-match level-ups and a "top 3 today" board. |
| **Vampire Survivors / Survivor.io** | Reverse bullet hell power fantasy: weak start, screen-filling end; XP gems you pick up; level-up choice is the main decision. | High for the feel: collectable XP orbs that burst out of kills, a vacuum magnet, louder level-ups. |
| **Brawl Stars** (Starr Drops) | Small rewards for the 1st, 4th and 8th win of the day; the drop "upgrades" its rarity on taps; odds published (50 / 28 / 15 / 5 / 2 %). | High: a daily "first wins" drop earned in play, odds shown (fits rule 5 because it is never sold). |
| **Diablo IV** | The open world scales to your level; regions have only a minimum level, so you pick where to go; world tiers raise risk and reward; rotating hot zones (Helltide, Whispers bounties). | High: the main answer to "the world is linear". |
| **Path of Exile (Atlas)** | The endgame is a map you choose routes through and upgrade. | Medium: a late-game atlas of our 29 areas with modifiers. |
| **Zelda: Breath of the Wild** | "Triangle rule": hills hide what is behind them so you keep wondering; towers show you the land and you choose a goal; heatmaps showed where players got bored. | High for layout: landmarks visible from far, side valleys, secrets behind ridges. |
| **RuneScape** | Skills trained anywhere (farming, mining, fishing) give a reason to visit old areas; farming patches spread over the map; long-term "99" goals. | High: farm patches and gathering spots in every biome. |
| **Hay Day** | Plant, wait, harvest, sell: every return session pays something; no forced tutorial; neighbourhood derbies kept players. | High for the home farm. |
| **Genshin Impact (Serenitea Pot)** | A personal realm you decorate; placing furniture raises a comfort value that speeds a passive currency; friends visit and help once a day. | High: the farm as a home you show friends. |
| **Albion Online (islands)** | Personal islands you upgrade; crops and animals; each biome gives a bonus for a different crop, so players trade and plan. | Medium: biome seeds that only grow well at home after you visit that biome. |
| **Stardew Valley** | Seasons and a calendar give small goals each day; gifts and festivals. | Medium: a weekly farm festival / weekly order board. |

## 3. Ideas, grouped

### A. Rewarding feel in the fight (quick wins, no new system)
1. **XP and loot burst out of kills** as pixel orbs and coins that fly to the hero (vacuum radius grows with level).
   Big kills (elite, lair boss) shower more. Today XP just appears.
2. **Louder level-ups:** a short slow-motion flash, the hero's own pixel burst, a sound sting and the new number big
   on screen for one second (no text block).
3. **Combo / streak counter:** kills within 3 s chain into a counter that raises gold and drop chance a little;
   breaking it is fine. Gives fast thumb action a visible score.
4. **Short goals on the HUD:** one tiny chip ("Boars 7/10") from a rotating list of 3 bounties per area, finished in
   2-5 minutes, paying a small chest. Replaces nothing, adds a reason for the next minute.
5. **Daily first wins:** a drop for the 1st, 3rd and 6th finished activity of the day (bounty, den clear, dungeon,
   Showdown win), rarity upgraded on taps, odds always shown. Earned only, never sold.
6. **Rare world moments:** a 1-in-N "treasure goblin" style runner that drops a lot if you catch it; a shiny monster
   variant with a collection log entry.

### B. Run-based choices (Archero / Vampire Survivors)
7. **Pick 1 of 3 boons** at each hall in party dungeons and each floor of the Rift, themed per hero kit (more
   bounces, bigger super, a pet that fights for the run). Boons last only for that run. Party dungeons need them seeded
   (rule 8: from the leader's seed).
8. **Showdown in-match upgrades:** power cubes already exist; let a cube offer a 1-of-3 choice instead of a flat stat.

### C. A home outside the world: the Homestead (farm)
A calm island or valley you travel to with a lodestone, separate from the arena and the Bastion (which stays the
tower defense). Unlock around level 8-12 (after Bank & Camp), with a "New" pulse.

- **Crop patches** (start with 3, more with Homestead level): plant seeds, they grow in real time (15 min to 8 h),
  harvest by walking over them. Seeds drop from monsters in each biome, so the world feeds the farm.
- **What the farm gives** (world bonuses only, never arena power):
  - cooking: meals that give a 30-minute world buff (XP, gold, ore luck),
  - potions/dyes and crafting inputs for the Forge (biome herbs instead of only ore),
  - pet food: pets grow and unlock their world bonuses faster,
  - decorations bought with farm coins.
- **Animals** from the pets system live there (pets already give world bonuses only).
- **Comfort value** (Serenitea Pot idea): placed decorations raise it, comfort speeds the farm's passive output.
- **Friends visit** with a code (like Bastion worlds): water a friend's crops once a day, both get a small bonus.
- **Order board** (Hay Day): 3 orders refresh every few hours ("6 frost berries + 2 ember peppers"), paying chests.
- **Biome seeds** (Albion idea): rare seeds from far biomes grow special crops, a reason to visit them.
- **Art:** real models only (Quaternius Stylized Nature / Farm and Medieval Village kits are CC0: crops, fences,
  barns, animals). The ground uses the existing painted tiles.
- **Save:** a new `profile.home` through `sanitizeProfile` with defaults; old saves get an empty farm.

### D. A non-linear world
Options, from small to big:

1. **Bounties spread back across the map (small).** Each day 3 areas you already passed are "hot": bonus XP scaled
   to your level, a better chest, seed drops. Old areas get a reason to return without a redesign.
2. **Level-scaled areas after the first biome (medium, D4 style).** Woodlands stays the tutorial (1-30). After it,
   every biome has only a **minimum** level and scales its monsters to your level (with a floor), so after 30 you
   choose any of the open biomes. Rewards follow the monster level, so no area is "dead". World Tiers raise both.
3. **Hub and spokes (big).** Base Camp becomes a crossroads: Woodlands south, then roads west (Mountains), east
   (Volcanic), north (Arcane), with Ocean and Celestial opened by bosses. Each road keeps its areas in order inside the
   biome (they are designed that way), but the player picks the biome. Needs a new layout in `11-world-data.js`,
   minimap, world map, lodestones and a save migration (positions, waypoints, furthest-area stat).
4. **Atlas endgame (later, PoE style).** At 210, a map of all 29 areas with modifiers you roll and run for keys,
   Titan Souls and set pieces.
5. **Layout ideas (BotW):** landmarks visible from far that pull you sideways, side valleys with secrets behind
   ridges (the existing secrets are a start), shortcuts that open once found.

## 4. Recommended order

1. **Feel first (A1-A4):** orbs that fly to you, louder level-ups, a streak counter, area bounties on a HUD chip.
   Small, safe, every session feels better. One PR.
2. **Hot zones (D1)** plus **daily first wins (A5).** Gives a reason to go back without moving the map.
3. **Homestead (C)** as its own system behind a PR Casper approves (new save block, new models).
4. **Level-scaled biomes (D2)** after 30, then decide if the full hub-and-spokes layout (D3) is still needed.
5. **Run boons (B7)** for dungeons and Rift.

Questions for Casper before building: farm on its own island, or a valley next to Base Camp? Should the world be
fully free after level 30 (D2), or keep the road and only add hot zones (D1)?
